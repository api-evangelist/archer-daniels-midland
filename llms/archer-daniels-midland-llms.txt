# ADM (Archer Daniels Midland)

> ADM is a global leader in human and animal nutrition,
> unlocking the power of nature to enrich the quality of life.

## About

ADM (Archer Daniels Midland Company) is one of the world's largest agricultural
processors and food ingredient providers, founded in 1902 and headquartered in
Chicago, Illinois. The company operates across the full agricultural value chain —
from sourcing and processing raw commodities to delivering finished ingredients
and nutrition solutions to customers in over 200 countries and territories.
ADM employs approximately 40,000 people globally and operates more than
320 processing plants and 500 procurement facilities worldwide. The company
is publicly traded on the NYSE under the ticker symbol ADM and is a component
of the S&P 500.

ADM's business is organized into three primary segments: Ag Services & Oilseeds,
Carbohydrate Solutions, and Nutrition. Its customers include food and beverage
manufacturers, feed producers, pharmaceutical companies, and industrial
product makers.

## Products and Services

### Human Nutrition
Ingredients and formulated solutions for food and beverage manufacturers,
covering a wide range of product categories including:
- Acidulants
- Alternative & plant-based proteins (soy, pea, wheat)
- Beans, pulses & seeds
- Botanicals & herbal extracts
- Natural and synthetic colors
- Edible and specialty oils
- Flavors and flavor systems
- Flours & grains
- Food bases & marinades
- Microbiome solutions (probiotics, prebiotics, postbiotics)
- Peanuts & peanut products
- Specialty health solutions (omega-3s, vitamins, minerals)
- Starches
- Sweeteners (HFCS, dextrose, polyols, stevia)
- Texturants (hydrocolloids, fibers, emulsifiers)
- Formulated systems (complete ingredient systems by application)

Human Nutrition serves categories including: bakery, beverages, confectionery,
dairy & dairy alternatives, convenience foods, health & wellness, infant
nutrition, meat & meat alternatives, and snacks.

### Animal Nutrition
Complete feed ingredients, premixes, and nutrition solutions for livestock,
poultry, and aquaculture producers. Includes amino acids, vitamins, minerals,
enzymes, and specialty additives. Delivered through ADM's global network of
premix and blending facilities.

### Pet Nutrition
Ingredient solutions for pet food manufacturers, including proteins, fats,
fibers, and functional ingredients for companion animal health and wellness.

### Industrial Biosolutions
Biobased products and industrial solutions derived from agricultural feedstocks,
including starches, ethanol, and fermentation-derived specialty chemicals used
in industries such as packaging, adhesives, and personal care.

### Ag Services & Oilseeds
Global origination, merchandising, transportation, and processing of oilseeds
(soy, canola, sunflower, cottonseed) and agricultural commodities. Includes
crushing and refining operations that produce vegetable oils and protein meals.

### Carbohydrate Solutions
Corn wet and dry milling operations producing sweeteners, starches, ethanol,
and other corn-derived products for food, beverage, and industrial customers.

### Global Trade
Grain and oilseed trading, transportation logistics, and risk management
services, leveraging ADM's global network of port terminals, rail, truck,
and barge infrastructure.

## Sustainability

ADM's sustainability strategy is built around four pillars:
- **Climate**: Reducing Scope 1, 2, and 3 greenhouse gas emissions; targeting
  a 25% reduction in absolute GHG emissions by 2035.
- **Biodiversity & No Deforestation**: Responsible sourcing commitments to
  eliminate deforestation and conversion from supply chains for priority
  commodities including soy, palm, and wheat.
- **Regenerative Agriculture**: Supporting farmers in transitioning to
  regenerative practices through the Strive 35 program and other initiatives.
- **Human Rights**: Commitment to ethical labor practices throughout the
  supply chain, with policies aligned to the UN Guiding Principles on Business
  and Human Rights.

ADM publishes an annual Corporate Sustainability Report and reports in
alignment with GRI, SASB, TCFD, and CDP frameworks.

## Insights & Innovation

ADM provides thought leadership, consumer trend research, and co-innovation
services to help customers develop and differentiate their products.

- **Consumer Trends**: Annual OutsideVoice℠ research program tracking
  global consumer attitudes toward food, health, and sustainability.
- **Innovation Services**: Application labs, pilot plants, and formulation
  expertise across global innovation centers in North America, Europe,
  and Asia-Pacific.
- **Regulatory & Scientific Support**: Technical and regulatory expertise
  to help customers navigate ingredient approvals and labeling requirements
  across global markets.
